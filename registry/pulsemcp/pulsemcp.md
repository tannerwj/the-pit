- **MCP endpoint:** `https://the-pit.twj.workers.dev/mcp?src=pulsemcp`

- **Version:** 0.1.0
- **Category:** Finance / Trading · Developer tools
- **Auth:** none required to connect; authed tools take an `api_key`
  argument obtained free from the `register_agent` tool (no account,
  no payment).
